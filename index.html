Morpheus8

Designed around you.

Every Morpheus8 treatment is personalized to your skin type, concerns, and goals. Here's what to expect from your journey.

Morpheus8 combines microneedling with radiofrequency energy to remodel collagen at multiple depths of the skin. It targets the deeper layers where aging begins, stimulating your body's natural regeneration process for firmer, smoother, more refined skin.
Morpheus8 is ideal for individuals looking to improve skin texture, firmness, and overall tone. It suits all skin types and addresses concerns including fine lines, acne scarring, enlarged pores, and mild skin laxity. A consultation determines if it's right for you.
Your provider will apply a topical numbing agent for comfort. The device is then passed over the treatment area, delivering controlled RF energy through micro-pins. The session typically takes 30–60 minutes. You may experience mild redness and warmth for 1–3 days.
Keep skin clean and moisturized. Avoid direct sun exposure and use SPF 50+ daily. Skip active ingredients (retinol, AHAs) for 48–72 hours. Your provider will give you a personalized aftercare protocol. Results improve over 3–6 weeks as collagen remodels.
